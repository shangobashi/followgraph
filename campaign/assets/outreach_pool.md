# Outreach Pool Logic
## High-signal targets
- creators with 10K-200K followers who irregularly clean their following
- community/ecosystem operators who manage outreach networks
- growth operators researching prospect influence
- analytics/tool builders comparing X data tooling

## Conversation openers
1. "Mapped the follow network around [niche/community] and found accounts that sit between multiple clusters. Worth me sharing the export?"
2. "Your following count looks like data, not noise. I built a local scanner that exports it without asking for your password."
3. "Is your current 'who follows whom' workflow manual? I can share an alternative."

## Rules
- lead with value
- mention privacy/no-auth
- ask for one concrete reaction
